Documents and legal acts

The law under which IradLoto works, its licence, the rules of its games and the Enterprise's own orders. The rules in force are binding on players and retailers.

Report · 26 February 2026 · AR-2025

Annual report 2025

Published

Sales of the draw games 137.7 million som (23,122,000 lines); 50% to prizes, 20% (27.5 million som) to the Sport and Youth Fund, 10% to the ISEZ budget, 12% to retailers, 8% to costs. Chabysh and the Tyin series are reported separately.

Licence · 20 February 2012 · L-001

Lottery licence L-001

In force

Issued by the Ministry of Economy to the State Lottery Enterprise IradLoto to hold draw lotteries and issue instant tickets in the ISEZ; renewed every five years, last in 2022.

Act · 28 November 2011 · № 11-19

Gaming and Lotteries Act 2011

In force

Makes the state lottery and the Hippodrome tote the only legal games of chance in Irad, provides for a state lottery enterprise under licence, bans bookmaking, online gambling and sales to anyone under 18.
