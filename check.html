My tickets

Tickets you keep are saved in this browser only, not with IradLoto. After each draw they are checked here. A prize is paid only against the paper ticket.

By ticket number

The number is printed under the barcode: two letters, then 4, 4, 5 and 1 digits, for example AB-0760-1412-03321-7.

By your numbers

Choose the game and the draw, then mark the numbers on your ticket.

This check is for information. A prize is paid only against the paper ticket; the Draw Commission's record is the official result.

Claiming a prize over 5,000 som

Prizes up to 5,000 som are paid at any point of sale or post office. Larger prizes are claimed in person at the Prize Payment Office, Pochtamt Street 9, Talas, with the paper ticket, your identity card and the completed claim form. Prizes over 50,000 som are paid only by bank transfer.
