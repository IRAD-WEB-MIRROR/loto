29 July 2024

Two Altın Bayge jackpots in one month

For the first time two Altın Bayge jackpots have been won within a month: 3,215,000 som in draw No. 639 on 29 June, on a ticket from a lottery kiosk in Iyık Janyl, and 2,018,000 som in draw No. 643 on 27 July, on a ticket from a kiosk at Bazar-i Kalan, Talas.

Winners' names are published only with their written consent.
