Record jackpot of 6,325,000 som rolls down

The Altın Bayge jackpot reached 6,325,000 som, the largest in the game's history, for the must-be-won draw No. 761 on 31 October. Nobody matched all six numbers, so the jackpot rolled down: three tickets with five numbers win 1,117,508 som each and 263 tickets with four numbers 13,289 som each.

Holders of the winning five-number tickets should bring the ticket, their passport and bank details to the Prize Payment Office, Pochtamt Street 9. The jackpot for 7 November is 1,428,000 som.
