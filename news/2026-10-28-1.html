Draw calendar for 2027

In 2027 IradLoto will hold 52 Altın Bayge draws (every Saturday from 2 January to 25 December), 104 Joguru! draws (Wednesdays and Sundays), and a Tört Tuyak draw and a Chabysh race every day. Draws on public holidays are held as usual.

Draw times are unchanged: Altın Bayge and Joguru! at 20:30, Chabysh at 19:30, Tört Tuyak at 21:00.
