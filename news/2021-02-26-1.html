26 February 2021

2020 results: sales up despite the quarantine

In 2020 players bought 18,414,000 lines for 110.2 million som, the first year above 100 million. The Sport and Youth Fund received 22.0 million som.

Most of the growth came from Tört Tuyak, in its first full year of daily draws.
