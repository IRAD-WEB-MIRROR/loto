2025 results: 27.5 million som for the Sport and Youth Fund

In 2025 players bought 23,122,000 lines in the draw games for 137.7 million som, the highest sales since the lottery began. The Sport and Youth Fund received 27.5 million som and the ISEZ budget 13.8 million.
