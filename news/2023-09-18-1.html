18 September 2023

Retail network: new terminals in the districts

Twenty-six new terminals have been installed this summer, most of them in district post offices and Torgoy shops in Sary-Bulak and Lower Naryn districts. IradLoto now has some 1,100 points of sale.

Sellers at the new points were trained at the Retail Network Department in Talas. A list of points of sale by district is on the Where to buy page.

IradLoto press service
