Tyin Akyel: three Akyel horses among the prizes

The new Tyin Akyel series goes on sale on 20 October: 20 som a card, 450,000 cards printed. Each of the three top prizes is a two-year-old Akyel from the Akyel Syndicate's stud, with pedigree papers and a year's stabling paid, or 400,000 som instead.

The series is on sale until 31 May 2027 or until the cards run out. The Tyin Kış winter series (10 som) remains on sale.
