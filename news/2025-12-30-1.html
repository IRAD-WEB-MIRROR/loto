Tyin Bayram: the holiday series from 1 January

A new series of Tyin instant tickets, Tyin Bayram, goes on sale on 1 January 2026: 10 som a card, ten top prizes of 50,000 som, 500,000 cards printed. The series is on sale until 30 June 2026.

Draws are held as usual over the holidays; the office is closed on 1 and 2 January.
