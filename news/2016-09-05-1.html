5 September 2016

Altın Bayge: a jackpot of 4,707,000 som won in Nurzhan

In draw No. 231 on Saturday 3 September a single ticket bought at a Torgoy shop in Nurzhan matched all six numbers and wins the jackpot of 4,707,000 som, the largest Altın Bayge prize ever won by one ticket.

The winner has claimed the prize at the Prize Payment Office. Four tickets matched five numbers and win 22,890 som each. The jackpot for 10 September starts again from 1,000,000 som.
