19 December 2022

Altın Bayge jackpot of 3,574,000 som won at the Talas bazaar

Draw No. 559 on 17 December has one jackpot winner: the ticket was sold at an IradLoto kiosk at Bazar-i Kalan, Talas. It is the first Altın Bayge jackpot won outright since 2016.

The jackpot for the New Year draw on 31 December starts again from the guaranteed minimum.
