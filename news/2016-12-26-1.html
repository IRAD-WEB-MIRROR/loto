26 December 2016

Draws and office hours over the New Year

All draws will be held as scheduled over the New Year: Altın Bayge on Saturday 31 December at 20:30 and Joguru! on Sunday 1 January. The office and the Prize Payment Office are closed on 1 and 2 January.

Retailers set their own hours over the holiday; post offices sell tickets during their opening hours.
