15 August 2019

New game: Tört Tuyak, every evening from 1 September

IradLoto's third draw game, Tört Tuyak ("four hooves"), starts on 1 September 2019. Pick 4 numbers from 1 to 20; draws every day at 21:00. A line costs 3 som; all four numbers win a fixed 5,000 som, three numbers 50 som.

Slips are at every terminal from 26 August. The draw is shown on IradOne after the evening news.
