Sport and Youth Fund: school sports grounds in the districts

In 2024 IradLoto transferred 25.7 million som to the Sport and Youth Fund from 128.6 million som of draw-game sales. The Fund's grants for the year went to school sports grounds, fields for the horse games in the districts, kit for children's teams, the Irad League youth cup and the purses of the national horse games.

The full list of grants is in the Fund's annual report.

IradLoto press service
