How to apply

Applications are accepted until 17:00 on the closing date. Send a letter addressed to the Director General with a CV, a copy of your diploma and a copy of your passport to Pochtamt Street 9, Talas, or by e-mail to hr@loto.iradgov.org with the reference of the vacancy in the subject line.

Shortlisted candidates are invited to an interview within two weeks of the closing date. Staff of IradLoto may not play the Enterprise's games.
