Rules of the Chabysh lottery

In force

1. Chabysh is a draw lottery. Its twelve horses belong to IradLoto and do not run in real races. The finishing order of each race is drawn by the ball machine from twelve balls before the Draw Commission; the race shown afterwards follows the drawn order.

2. A race is drawn every day at 19:30. Sales close at 19:15. A bet costs 5 som.

3. The player chooses: the winner (pays 30 som); the first two in order (forecast, 330 som); or the first three in order (tricast, 3,300 som). Prizes are fixed and do not depend on the number of winners.

4. In other respects the general rules of IradLoto draws and the Procedure for the payment of prizes apply.
