Order · 20 March 2012 · No. 14-p

Rules of the Altın Bayge lottery

In force, as amended 2019

1. The player chooses 6 numbers from 1 to 45 in each line. A line costs 10 som; a slip holds up to 10 lines and may be played for up to 8 consecutive draws. The terminal may choose the numbers (quick pick).

2. Draws are held on Saturdays at 20:30. Sales for a draw close at 20:00. The printed ticket is the only proof of participation.

3. Half of the sales of each draw forms the prize fund. Three numbers win a fixed 20 som. The rest of the fund is shared: 45% to the jackpot, 35% to four numbers and 20% to five numbers, divided equally among the winning lines of each tier.

4. The jackpot starts at 1,000,000 som. A tier nobody wins is added to the next jackpot. When the jackpot reaches 6,000,000 som the draw is a must-be-won draw: if no line matches six numbers, the jackpot is shared half and half between the lines with five and with four numbers.

5. Prizes are paid against the ticket within 180 days of the draw, at the places set by the Procedure for the payment of prizes. Unclaimed prizes return to the prize fund of later draws.
