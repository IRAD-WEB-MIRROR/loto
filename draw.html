The draw

The draws are held in the IradLoto studio at Pochtamt Street 9 under the eye of the Draw Commission: Altın Bayge on Saturdays and Joguru! on Sundays and Wednesdays at 20:30, Tört Tuyak every day at 21:00. Watch the machine here at the draw hour.

The numbers shown are the official result of the draw. Results, prizes and the draw protocol are published on the results page ten minutes after each draw.
