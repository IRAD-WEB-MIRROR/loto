2023

  • 18 September 2023
    Retail network: new terminals in the districts

    Twenty-six new terminals have been installed this summer, most of them in district post offices and Torgoy shops in Sary-Bulak and Lower Naryn districts. IradLoto now has some 1,100 points of sale.

  • 27 February 2023
    New game: Chabysh, the horse-race lottery, from 11 March

    From 11 March 2023 IradLoto draws a race every evening at 19:30 between twelve horses that belong to the lottery. The ball machine draws the finishing order before the Draw Commission, and the race is then shown to match it.

2021

  • 26 February 2021
    2020 results: sales up despite the quarantine

    In 2020 players bought 18,414,000 lines for 110.2 million som, the first year above 100 million. The Sport and Youth Fund received 22.0 million som.

2020

  • 20 March 2020
    Draws continue; office receives claims by appointment

    Under the quarantine regime the draws continue in the IradOne studio without guests; the member of the public on the Draw Commission takes part by video link and signs the record the next morning.

2019

  • 15 August 2019
    New game: Tört Tuyak, every evening from 1 September

    IradLoto's third draw game, Tört Tuyak ("four hooves"), starts on 1 September 2019. Pick 4 numbers from 1 to 20; draws every day at 21:00. A line costs 3 som; all four numbers win a fixed 5,000 som, three numbers 50 som.

2018

  • 4 June 2018
    Two new ball sets brought into service

    Two new sets of draw balls were weighed and measured by the Iradstandart metrology laboratory and sealed before the Draw Commission on 1 June. They replace the two sets in use since 2012.

2017

  • 20 November 2017
    Responsible play: new rules for retailers

    From 1 December 2017 retailers must ask for an identity document from any buyer who looks under 25, may not sell on credit and must display the helpline number at every terminal (order No. 9-p of 20 November 2017).

  • 1 March 2017
    2016 results: 15.3 million som for the Sport and Youth Fund

    In 2016 players bought 9,739,000 lines in the draw games for 76.3 million som. Half went to prizes, and the Sport and Youth Fund received 15.3 million som.

2016

  • 26 December 2016
    Draws and office hours over the New Year

    All draws will be held as scheduled over the New Year: Altın Bayge on Saturday 31 December at 20:30 and Joguru! on Sunday 1 January. The office and the Prize Payment Office are closed on 1 and 2 January.

  • 5 September 2016
    Altın Bayge: a jackpot of 4,707,000 som won in Nurzhan

    In draw No. 231 on Saturday 3 September a single ticket bought at a Torgoy shop in Nurzhan matched all six numbers and wins the jackpot of 4,707,000 som, the largest Altın Bayge prize ever won by one ticket.
