Leadership

IradLoto is a state enterprise owned by the ISEZ and accountable to the Ministry of Economy. It is run by the Director General with two deputies; every draw is held before the Draw Commission, which is independent of the Enterprise's management.

Director General

Director General

Head of the State Lottery Enterprise IradLoto

Directs the Enterprise, signs its orders and the rules of its games, and reports to the Ministry of Economy on sales, prizes and the transfers to the Sport and Youth Fund and the ISEZ budget.
