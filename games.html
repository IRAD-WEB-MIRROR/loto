Games

Three draw games and the Tyin instant tickets. All tickets are sold over the counter only.

Altın Bayge 6/45

Irad's big Saturday game. Pick 6 numbers from 1 to 45. The jackpot starts at 1,000,000 som and grows until somebody wins it; when it reaches 6,000,000 som the draw is a "must be won" draw.

10 som a line, up to 10 lines on a slip. Draws on Saturdays at 20:30 on IradOne; sales close at 20:00.

Half of the money played goes into prizes. Three numbers pay a fixed 20 som; the rest of the pool is shared: 45% to the jackpot, 35% to four numbers and 20% to five. A tier nobody wins goes into the next jackpot. In a must-be-won draw with no winner of six, the jackpot is shared half and half between the fives and the fours.

Joguru! 5/36

Twice a week, Wednesdays and Sundays. Pick 5 numbers from 1 to 36. Named after the cry "Joguru!" ("up!"), the one everybody knows from Tulpar.

5 som a line. Draws at 20:30; the jackpot starts at 100,000 som and rolls over until it is won (must be won at 2,000,000 som).

Tört Tuyak 4/20

Every day at 21:00. Pick 4 numbers from 1 to 20; fixed prizes: all four 5,000 som, three 50 som.

3 som a line. The name means "four hooves": four numbers, every evening, like the horses at the Hippodrome.

Chabysh horse race

Twelve horses, every evening at 19:30. 5 som a bet: the winner pays 30 som, the first two in order 330 som, the first three in order 3,300 som.

Tyin instant tickets

Scratch off the panel and see at once if you have won. The number of prizes in each series is fixed before it is printed.

Try a Tyin Akyel card

A demo card to show how it works: scratch the silver panel with your mouse or finger. Three matching symbols win that prize. There is no real prize here, and real cards are sold only over the counter.

Prizes up to 1,000 som are paid where you bought the ticket. A tyin is the hundredth part of a som, and the coin in the old horseback game.

How to play

  1. Take a slip at any IradLoto retailer, or ask for a quick pick and the terminal chooses for you.
  2. Mark your numbers in each line you want to play and the number of draws (up to 8).
  3. Pay and keep the printed ticket: it is the only proof of your bet. Check it is for the right draw.
  4. Watch the draw on IradOne, or check your ticket here or at any terminal.
