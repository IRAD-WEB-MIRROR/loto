Waiting to be claimed

Prizes must be claimed within 180 days of the draw. Unclaimed money goes back into the prize fund of later draws.

Jackpot winners

Claiming a prize over 1,000 som

Prizes up to 1,000 som are paid at any IradLoto terminal on showing the ticket. Prizes from 1,001 to 50,000 som are paid at the main post offices in Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl and at the district post offices, on the ticket and your passport. Prizes over 50,000 som are claimed in person at the Prize Payment Office, Pochtamt Street 9, Talas, with the paper ticket, your identity card and the completed claim form, and are paid only by bank transfer.
