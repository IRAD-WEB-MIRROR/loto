Where to buy and how to claim

More than 1,100 points of sale in every city and district of Irad.

Where to buy

  • Every Irad Pochtasy post office, at the counter (about 190 offices).
  • Bereke supermarkets at the tills; Jıldız Market in Nurzhan; Torgoy shops.
  • IradLoto kiosks: the Great Bazaar in Talas, Radiobazar in Nurzhan, Iyık Janyl Bazaar, bus stations, and the Hippodrome on race days.
  • Look for the purple IradLoto sign with the gold stripe. Only licensed retailers may sell tickets.

Prizes must be claimed within 180 days of the draw. A torn or washed ticket can still be paid if the barcode and the number can be read; bring it to the IradLoto office.

No sales on the internet

Under the Gaming and Lotteries Act 2011, lottery tickets may be sold only over the counter by licensed retailers, and online betting is illegal. IradLoto has no app and does not sell through Tulpar or any website. If someone offers you IradLoto tickets online, it is a fraud: call +996-39-2-25-55-55.
